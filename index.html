Fair questions

Why go here instead of asking my AI directly?

Your AI will happily chat about your week for twenty minutes and you will leave with nothing. This site imposes structure: five areas, one answer, one move, one prompt. It is the difference between journaling and being asked a direct question. Your AI also only knows what you have told it, it agrees with you too easily, and it may not reliably follow up unless you give it a time and a clear instruction.

Is the check-in tested?

Honestly: the five-question structure is common sense, not science. What the research does support is the mechanic underneath it: weekly check-ins help people follow through. This site is new. We will publish what we learn, starting with one number: how many people say they made their move.

Do I need an account? What do you store?

No account, no email, no tracking of who you are. Your answers stay in your own browser. We count anonymous events (started, finished, copied the prompt, came back) so we can tell if the thing works. That is it.

Is this medical or mental health advice?

No. This is a mirror, not a diagnosis and not treatment. If you are struggling, talk to someone you trust or a professional.

Why weekly?

A week is short enough to act and long enough to matter. Resolutions die by February. A week is a bet you can win.
